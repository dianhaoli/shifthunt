## 3. How the thesis moved (one line per phase)

1. **Budget-as-test environment (d1–d7, PAPER_BEST).** Every draft shared the same core numbers (6/24 → 23/24; κ = 0.002) and the same limits (six items, three rank-2 traps); they differed in framing [R2 §1 "Why the winner won"].
2. **"Verification without search" (PAPER_BEST → V4).** The title phrase was kept because the brief called it the author's framing; reviewers flagged it because Terra does search (11/12) [RM §E]. V3_r1 retreated to "searches one step further" [abs].
3. **Instrument validity (V5).** The judge became a measured instrument; agents remained co-equal [CL5 item 3].
4. **The judge needs the key (V6).** The judge ladder leads, and agents become the testbed [CL6 §1].
5. **Commissions vs omissions (V7).** A two-part measured claim replaced V6's causal one [CL7 §1].

The judge result was present from d1 onward: report-judge κ = 0.002 is in every abstract from d1 to V2 [abs]. "A report cannot certify an omission", the core of V7, first appears in PAPER_BEST line 141, grafted from d1 [R1 §1]. d5's "nothing found is the error the judge credits (41/44)" is the direct ancestor of V7's omission half [d5 abs].
